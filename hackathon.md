# All Gas Hackathon log

App: **Opportunity Concierge**

## Human direction
- Product thesis: turn a public opportunity URL into an evidence-backed go/no-go decision and concrete next actions.
- Priority: everyday usefulness, not a developer dashboard.

## AI-assisted implementation
- Initial schema, mutations, dashboard scaffold and provider boundaries were authored with ChatGPT/Codex assistance on 2026-09-06.
- External provider integrations are intentionally behind narrow adapters so their real SDK/API behavior can be verified before activation.
